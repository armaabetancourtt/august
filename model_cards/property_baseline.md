# Model Card — Property Baseline v0

## Purpose

Transparent, deterministic benchmark for property fair-value analysis.

## Status

Foundation baseline. **Not trained and not a production valuation model.**

## Inputs

Area, bedrooms, bathrooms, neighborhood price/m² anchor, inflation, mortgage rate, demand and supply indices.

## Evaluation

No public MAE / MAPE is reported yet because the baseline has not been evaluated on a versioned real-property holdout.

## Limitations

Missing exact location, building age, floor, parking, amenities, quality, transaction-vs-listing distinction and calibrated uncertainty.

## Why keep it?

A sophisticated model must demonstrate measurable improvement over an interpretable baseline.
